How Pollution Operates in Kenya

Simple, vector-style breakdowns of climate feedback loops impacting our soil, rivers, and atmosphere.

System Breakdown #01

The Urban Water Runoff Loop

During heavy rains, uncollected plastic debris blocks open channels, forcing contaminated water into informal housing zones and raising waterborne pathogens by 300%.

Blocked Drain → Stagnant Flooding
E. coli Contamination Spread
System Breakdown #02

Deforestation & Siltation Cycle

Removing tree canopy along major water towers causes topsoil erosion. Heavy silt washes into hydro-reservoirs, drastically reducing clean water holding capacity.

Topsoil Loss → River Turbidity
Reservoir Capacity drops 40%
System Breakdown #03

Urban Heat Island Effect

Asphalt and concrete absorb daytime solar heat, radiating it back at night. Without tree shade, urban neighborhoods stay up to 4.5°C warmer than surrounding rural areas.

Concrete Heat Trapping
High Energy & Thermal Stress
